If you lack certain documents, include a written application explaining the situation along with your other documents. This does not constitute an exemption from required documents; however, SFAO may assess the situation keeping in view your written application.

Here are some alternatives:

  • Bank Statement: If your family receives financial support from abroad, provide a bank statement for the last six months showing the credited funds.
  • Utility Bills: If your family's income comes from a privately owned business, attach an application detailing the source of income and include a utility bill from the business.
  • Affidavit:
    • For Drivers: Submit a written application and an affidavit on stamp paper detailing the source of income and monthly salary.
    • For Agriculture: Provide an affidavit on stamp paper if the income is from agriculture.
  • Applications: If the employee does not receive a salary, submit an application clearly stating the employer's legal status and clarifying the situation.
