Need-Cum-Merit Based Scholarships — Zakat & Ushr Department, Government of Sindh (2025–26)

The Government of Sindh, Auqaf & Religious Affairs, Zakat & Ushr Department is offering Need-cum-Merit Based Scholarships out of Zakat funds for the deserving regular students of this University. All students of Morning and Evening Programs are eligible to apply on a prescribed form. The scholarships provide need-based financial aid for education to deserving Muslim students.

Key Eligibility Criteria:
  • • Religion & Residency: Applicant must be a Muslim citizen of Pakistan with permanent residency in Sindh Province.
  • • Enrollment: Must be enrolled as a regular student at this University (Morning or Evening Program).
  • • Financial Need (Istehqaq): A District Zakat & Ushr Committee-attested Istehqaq Certificate is mandatory, confirming the applicant is financially deserving.
  • • Academic Standing: Candidates must demonstrate merit; a minimum CGPA of 2.5 is generally required.
  • • Employment Status: The applicant or their family members must not be government employees.
  • • Other Restrictions: Applicants cannot be receiving scholarships from other organizations simultaneously.
Application Procedure:

Interested students of Morning & Evening Programs are directed to download and fill in the prescribed scholarship application form available at: www.uok.edu.pk/sfao/scholarships.php
