FAQS

Q: What is 100 seconds service?
A: Subscribers can either win instantly (Mobile Phone) by answering all 10 questions correctly or earn points through gameplay for a chance to win monthly prize (Motorbike). The service charges will be deducted from the user’s mobile balance. The monthly prize is based on monthly calendar.
Q: How do I register to this service?

A: Register now by texting SUB to short code 2100, USSD to *2100# or via www.z.100seconds.com and enjoy a 3-day trial period with access to two credits (10 questions each).

After the trial, a daily fee of PKR 7 + applicable taxes applies /auto renewal, granting two credits (each credit valid for one game of 10 questions). Users may purchase extra credits (2 credits for PKR 7 + taxes, valid 24 hours) through the service website to play more games and accumulate additional points.

Q: If service is for Prepaid, Postpaid and corporate customers?
A: All Zong Prepaid Data Customers.
Q: What will be the suspension period of this service?
A: User can only be suspended from accessing the service, if the user does not charge for 60 consecutive days.
Q: How can I play additional rounds if I run out of credit?
A: Additional chances can be purchased in bundles of 2 for 7 PKR plus applicable taxes, both during your free trial and after. This is a one-time charge.
Q: Does 100 Seconds offers a Free Trial?
A: Yes, 100 Seconds offers a one-time 3-day free trial upon first-time registration. During the trial, users can play one game per day with two free chances. No charges apply unless the user continues beyond the trial or purchases extra chances. The trial ends automatically after three days or immediately upon unsubscription, and cannot be reactivated.
Q: Is there any limitation on free trial?
A: Yes, the free trial is a one-time offer per user and cannot be reactivated upon re-subscription.
Q: How to opt out from the service?
A: Subscription can be canceled by dialing *2100# or sending the keyword UNSUB to the short code 2100 or via the designated section on the web link.
