Sindh HEC Indigenous Scholarship (M.Phil / Ph.D.)

The Sindh HEC Indigenous Scholarship provides financial assistance for M.Phil and Ph.D. students enrolled in public sector universities across Sindh. The program aims to promote local research and empower postgraduate students to contribute to the academic and scientific development of the province.

Eligibility Criteria:
  • • Must be a Pakistani national and full-time research fellow of this university.
  • • Synopsis must be approved by ASRB and CGPA should be 3.0 and above.
  • • Must not be availing any other scholarship.
  • • Must not be full-time employed.
  • • Must not be a beneficiary of the Prime Minister Fee Reimbursement Scheme.
  • • Must not have been awarded this scholarship during the last 4 years.
