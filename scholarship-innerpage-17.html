Benazir Income Support Program (BISP) — HEC Need-Based Undergraduate Scholarship

The Benazir Income Support Program (BISP) collaborates with the Higher Education Commission (HEC) to provide need-based undergraduate scholarships to students in public sector universities, including the University of Karachi. These scholarships — often under the Ehsaas banner — aim to support financially disadvantaged students in continuing their education by covering tuition and related expenses.

Key Details:
  • • Scholarship Access: BISP-linked scholarships, such as the HEC Needs-Based Scholarship Program, are available for University of Karachi students. The 2025–26 session details are being updated by the Student Financial Aid Office.
  • • Targeted Aid: The program specifically targets students from low-income families to help with tuition and academic expenses.
  • • Eligibility: Must be a student enrolled in a public sector university and belong to a low-income household. Documentation of financial status is required.
  • • For Updates: Students are advised to visit the official SFAO scholarship page regularly for announcements regarding the 2025–26 session: www.uok.edu.pk/sfao/scholarships.php
